# Source and asset ownership

Original Parcel source, illustration, procedural icon/sample artwork, preview design and sound were created for this project. They are supplied for the commissioning user's development and commercial deployment. No separate open-source licence is imposed on that original work in this handoff. Set the company's chosen copyright/licensing terms before publishing a public repository.

ZIPFoundation and the browser-preview fflate dependency retain their respective MIT licences. Preserve the bundled notices when redistributing them. Apple framework/system-font use follows Apple's platform terms. The visual direction uses an original paper-and-garden character, with no film stills, studio characters, copied competitor graphics or external stock assets.
